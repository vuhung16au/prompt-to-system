---
id: termination-condition
title: "Termination Condition"
summary: "The criteria that dictate when an autonomous agent should stop reasoning and return a final answer."
---
Without clear termination conditions, agents may run indefinitely or stop prematurely. Conditions can be explicit (e.g., reaching a maximum number of steps) or determined by the model (e.g., outputting a specific `<DONE>` token).
