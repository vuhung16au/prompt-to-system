---
id: semantic-search
title: "Semantic Search"
summary: "A search technique that matches the meaning and intent of a query rather than relying on exact keyword matches."
aliases: ["vector search", "embedding search"]
---
Semantic search typically uses text embeddings to convert queries and documents into high-dimensional vectors. A vector database then calculates the distance between these vectors to find documents that are conceptually similar to the query, forming the backbone of most RAG systems.
