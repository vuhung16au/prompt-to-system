---
id: lost-in-the-middle
title: "Lost in the Middle"
summary: "A limitation of LLMs where they struggle to retrieve information located in the middle of a long context window."
aliases: ["U-shaped performance"]
---
Research shows that models are best at recalling information placed at the very beginning or the very end of their prompt. Important instructions or critical context should be placed accordingly to mitigate the 'lost in the middle' effect.
