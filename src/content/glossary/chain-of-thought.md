---
id: chain-of-thought
title: "Chain of Thought (CoT)"
summary: "A prompting technique where the model is instructed to break down its reasoning into intermediate steps."
aliases: ["CoT", "step-by-step thinking"]
---
Chain of Thought (CoT) prompting significantly improves the reasoning capabilities of Large Language Models. By asking the model to 'think step-by-step', it generates intermediate reasoning steps before arriving at a final answer, reducing errors in logic, math, and complex problem-solving.
